About

ON SITE

Kyle Beene is a play-by-play broadcaster based in the Blacksburg and Roanoke region with experience covering hockey, baseball, softball, and esports.

As a graduate from Virginia Tech's Sports Media and Analytics program and the Virginia Tech Graduate School, Kyle brings extensive experience with four years of sports broadcasting and five years in news media.

Kyle's broadcasting experience includes work with the Morehead City Marlins (CPL), High Point Hushpuppies (ONSL), and the Roanoke Rail Yard Dawgs (SPHL). He currently serves as Director of Broadcasting for Virginia Tech Hockey (ACHA), leading live game coverage and content production.
